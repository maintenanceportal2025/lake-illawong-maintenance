📋 Facility Booking Guidelines

🎯 How to Use the Booking System

To Make a Booking:

  1. Go to the Book tab
  2. Select your facility (Clubhouse, Gazebo, or Rec Room)
  3. Choose your date and time
  4. Enter your contact details and purpose
  5. Click Check Availability, then Submit Booking
  6. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's processed

To Edit a Booking:

  1. Go to the My Bookings tab
  2. Click on your booking
  3. Click Edit and make your changes

To Cancel a Booking:

  1. Go to My Bookings tab
  2. Click on your booking
  3. Click Cancel

🏢 Facility Information

🏛️ Clubhouse

  • Capacity: Maximum 30 people
  • Features: Full kitchen, tables and chairs, air conditioning
  • Best for: Parties, meetings, family gatherings
  • Operating Hours: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily
  • Quiet Hours: 8:00 PM - 8:00 AM (no amplified music after 8:00 PM)

🌳 Gazebo

  • Capacity: Up to 25 people
  • Features: BBQ facilities, picnic tables, outdoor setting
  • Best for: Outdoor events, BBQs, casual gatherings
  • Operating Hours: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily
  • Quiet Hours: 8:00 PM - 8:00 AM (be mindful of nearby residents)
  • Note: Weather dependent - no refunds for rain

🎮 Rec Room (Stage 2)

  • Capacity: Up to 15 people
  • Features: Pool table, TV, lounge area
  • Best for: Casual get-togethers, games nights
  • Operating Hours: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM daily

📜 Booking Policies

Fair Use:

Facilities are available to all residents on a first-come, first-served basis. You may make multiple bookings but please be considerate of other residents.

Operating Hours:

Clubhouse: 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM
Gazebo: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
Rec Room: 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM
All facilities must be vacated by closing time.

Cancellation Policy:

Bookings can be cancelled at any time through the "My Bookings" tab. Please cancel as early as possible if your plans change to allow others to use the facility.

Your Responsibility:

The person making the booking is responsible for:

  • The conduct of all guests
  • The condition of the facility during and after use
  • Ensuring all rules and guidelines are followed
  • Any damage caused during your booking period
