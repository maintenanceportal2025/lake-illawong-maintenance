How to Use Field Notifications

The three steps

  • 1. Choose who gets the message.
  • 2. Choose how: Email, SMS, or both.
  • 3. Write the message and press the Send button at the bottom. A confirmation box appears before anything goes out.

1. Choosing who gets it

  • All Units: every resident.
  • Zone 1 to 5: only the units in that zone. You can tick more than one zone.
  • E-Copy Recipients: only the people who hold the E-Copy role. A list of them appears so you can check it first.
  • Select Individual Units: pick particular people. Units with two residents list each person, so you can send to one and not the other. The Send button names who it is going to.
  • Clear Selections starts again.

2. Choosing how: Email, SMS, or both

  • Email only: the full message, with attachments if you add them.
  • SMS only: a short text message. Type whatever you like, but keep it under about 160 characters or it is sent as more than one text (the counter and a warning will tell you). Best for urgent alerts.
  • Email and SMS both ticked: the email carries your full message, and the SMS is always the same fixed text: "Lake Illawong: Please check your email for a new message." You do not type an SMS. Use this when the message is too long for a text, such as minutes or a notice with an attachment.
SMS only reaches residents who have the SMS-Alerts tag and a mobile number on file. Email only reaches residents who have an email address. If someone has no email, a warning box lists them so you can phone them.

3. Writing the message

  • Pick a Quick Template to fill in wording, or write your own.
  • Each person is greeted by first name automatically ("Hi Tony,"). Tick Custom Salutation to use one greeting for everyone instead, such as "Dear Residents,".
  • Use Alert Format adds the formal header and footer. Untick it for a plain email, which then asks you for an email subject.
  • Attach Files (PDF, Word, JPG, PNG, TXT; 10MB per file, 20MB total) go with the email, not the SMS.

Test Run (check before you send)

  • Once you have chosen who, how and written the message, press Test Run. Nothing is sent and nothing is counted against the email limit.
  • The results screen shows how many emails and texts would go, a sample of the email as the first person would see it, each person's greeting, the text message, and anyone who would get neither (so you can phone them).
  • It also says whether the send fits in the email limit. Gmail allows about 100 emails in 24 hours; if a send is too big for what is left, it is refused before anything goes out, and you can send to fewer zones and send the rest later.
  • Send me a real test copy sends ONE real email (and one text, if SMS is ticked) to your own test address and mobile, never to a resident. The subject starts with [TEST]. It uses 1 of the daily emails. With attachments, the test can take a little while.
  • The line above the Send button shows how many emails have been sent in the last 24 hours and how many are left. Texts are not affected by it.

Common situations

  • Send the AGM minutes to the E-Copy people: select E-Copy Recipients, tick Email and SMS, tick Custom Salutation if you want "Dear Residents,", write your covering note, attach the PDF, press Test Run to check it, then send.
  • Urgent alert to everyone: select All Units, tick SMS only, type the message, and send. Add Email as well only if you also want a fuller email (the SMS then becomes the fixed "check your email" text).
  • A note to one person: Select Individual Units, tick just that person.
